# World Development mock referee memo — 3 November 2026

**Recommendation: revise before submission; do not reopen the design search.** This is an internal editorial judgment, not a prediction of a journal decision. The strongest paper distinguishes three objects that are often conflated: material conditions in a targeted place, the circumstances of people originally observed there, and the acknowledgment of harm. Peru's shared-project delivery channel gives this distinction substance. The local estimates discipline what can be said about material outcomes and mobility; they do not show that the program failed, that mobility increased, or that reparative recognition changed.

The companion [claim-to-exhibit checklist](WORLD_DEVELOPMENT_CLAIM_CHECKLIST_2026-11-03.md) records what supports the claims. The [exact edit proposal](proposals/2026-11-03-world-development-referee-edits.md) is for review only.

## Prioritized referee comments

1. **Major — Put the scientific contribution ahead of the migration point estimate.** The abstract gives the individual movement estimate prominence even though its interval spans a negative contrast, zero, and a large positive contrast. The paper already qualifies this explicitly; the problem is emphasis, not an incorrect number. Lead with the collective rather than individual delivery channel and why origin-place and linked-person outcomes differ. Keep the movement estimate in the abstract as an illustration of uncertainty and selection, not the result to explain. Close when the abstract, introduction and conclusion all tell that same story. See proposed edit 1.
2. **Major — Make the scope of a receipt-effect interpretation immediate.** Section 4 has the correct ingredients: a documented B/C priority margin, cumulative receipt across changing allocation regimes, historical first-stage search, and selection qualifications. A reader should not have to assemble those ingredients from different paragraphs before encountering the main effect estimates. The 2012 rules support the relevance of the margin, not a stable instrument or a national queue from 2007 onward. Nor does fixing the selected geography retrospectively restore confirmatory coverage. Keep the reduced form and receipt ratio distinct; keep the latter conditional on the design and observation assumptions. Close when the transition into results states what the first stage can and cannot establish. See edits 2 and 3.
3. **Major — Make observation a result, not a footnote.** The estimated household availability jump (−29.71 percentage points) is about complete outcomes in source households, not a receipt effect on welfare. The wide linkage diagnostic does not show that selection is absent. Movement is observed only among linked people with valid canonical source and destination codes and known 2017 age ≥14; the seven other individual outcomes use a smaller complete-case population. “Adult” alone risks implying an age-18 restriction. Use the operational age threshold, make the lower-score-side 38.20% mean descriptive rather than a complier baseline, and keep directory totals separate from linked-cohort conditions. No defensible correction to all original residents is established. See edits 1 and 4.
4. **Major — Remove stale language about own-sample diagnostics.** Section 7 and Appendix D describe some ratios as awaiting diagnostics; the new appendix and registry now report own-sample diagnostics and their disposition. The warning against borrowing clearance is correct, and the new material does not promote any estimate. Replace the two prospective passages with a direct statement that the recorded gate and estimand apply separately to each row. Keep the three conventional AR exclusions visible, without recasting them as primary or familywise-adjusted evidence. See edit 5.
5. **Moderate — Preserve the extension hierarchy when shortening.** The pooled IV interaction is the receipt-heterogeneity test; sharp `rdhte` results concern assignment. A held interaction is not evidence of homogeneous effects. Endogenous project types are not exogenous versions of treatment. The employment association is between community-level measures and does not show an individual-level pathway. The current draft mostly gets this right: retain the explicit no-mediation paragraph and keep the larger signal inventory in Appendix E. Do not add a mediated fraction, a project-type causal ranking, or a comparison of subgroup significance as an interaction test.

## Contribution and scope

World Development's [publisher scope](https://shop.elsevier.com/journals/world-development/0305-750X) supports a multidisciplinary development framing. The [Banco de la República research record](https://investiga.banrep.gov.co/en/be-1236) provides a concrete comparison: linked administrative evidence on individual compensation in Colombia is relevant, but not a benchmark that shared projects in Peru must reproduce. Do not claim first-in-literature status or a cross-program effect comparison. The distinctions about recognition and welfare are conceptual; they are not measured impacts.

## Decision before more estimation

No new fit is necessary for the proposed revisions. The documented historical score, annual priority and award histories are unavailable under the agreed evidence boundary. Neither another geographic search nor a tighter bandwidth chosen for significance addresses that problem. If authors later want to make a stronger receipt-effect claim, they would need a separately justified bias-aware weak-identification design and an observation argument for that specific target population. Neither is a prerequisite for reporting the current bounded results honestly. The next production action, after edit approval, would be a scoped text patch, dependency and compilation checks, then a refresh of the internal collaborator/submission packet against the revised source.
